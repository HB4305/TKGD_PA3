| A. Objectives                                     | In this PA, students are asked to draw paper prototypes of the project and perform formative testing to evaluate their lo-fi prototype before creating the computer prototype in the next PA.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| B. Requirement 1: Paper prototype & storyboarding | Your group is asked to sketch various paper prototypes and corresponding storyboards conceptually illustrating your solutions for the top-priority problems identified in the previous PA. The group is asked to work on at least 2 main scenarios, and for each identified problem, your group is required to generate at least 3 different paper prototypes as solutions for it.<br><br>Your group must create YouTube video clips to demonstrate every paper prototype.<br><br>Your solutions should be new UIs / interaction designs as well as any new user workflow resulted by the new designs. Each sketch/prototype must be well explained. The requirements for your sketches include:<br>- A new solution for the identified problem.<br>- Explanation for the motivation of the sketched solutions/storyboard.<br>- What problem does it solve? How does it solve this?<br>- Reflections within the team about the strengths and weaknesses of the solutions.<br><br>For clarity, your group must use colored pens (not pencils) to draw on clean, unlined paper. |
| B. Requirement 2: Formative Testing               | Your group must develop a comprehensive formative testing plan to preliminarily test the lo-fi prototypes your team generated above. This plan outlines the testing objectives, target user groups, testing methods, and criteria for success. The plan should describe the specific tasks, scenarios, or functionalities that will be evaluated during the testing process.<br><br>After that, you need to conduct several testing sessions on 2-3 participants who do not have prior knowledge about the prototype you will test. All qualitative data including observations, feedback, and any issues encountered (points of improvement) during testing should be systematically documented in your report.<br><br>Choose the best lo-fi prototype for your group after analyzing the results of this testing and develop it into a hi-fi prototype in the next step.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| B. Requirement 3: Peer Review                     | Your group is required to present their work during lecture sessions, within a 5 to 10-minute timeframe. After the presentation, lecturers and other group members will provide feedback.<br><br>Groups must document the feedback and questions in a report, including the name of the commenter, the feedback or question, and the group's responses.<br><br>They will then have a number of days to revise the project requirements before submitting the final version on Moodle.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
